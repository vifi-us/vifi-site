A monthly subscription price is only the starting point when you compare AI receptionists. For a small service business, the useful number is the bill for the calls you expect the assistant to handle, plus the work your team still needs to do afterward.

Start with one ordinary week from your phone records. Count the calls you want covered, estimate how long the assistant would handle each one, and separate those calls from spam and calls your team will continue answering. Use a range if you do not yet know the volume.

## Start with the billing unit

A provider might charge for calls, minutes, or a combination of a monthly fee and usage. Ask what starts and stops the clock, whether each call is rounded, and what happens during a transfer.

ViFi measures the time its agent spends on the call, rounded up to a whole minute **for each call**. A call lasting 2 minutes 10 seconds uses 3 AI minutes. Fifty calls of that length use 150 AI minutes, even though the unrounded total is about 108 minutes.

Browser test calls and website widget conversations count too. Time talking to your team on your own phone after a transfer does not count toward ViFi AI minutes. These definitions are documented in [Plans and what's included](https://docs.vifi.us/billing/plans-and-pricing/).

## Compare the fee and extra usage together

For the published ViFi plans, calculate:

**Monthly fee + extra AI minutes × the plan's minute rate + extra eligible SMS segments × the segment rate.**

Subtract the included allowance from each usage total first. If usage stays below an allowance, the extra charge for that unit is zero. Keep minutes and SMS separate.

## Count text segments separately

A text message is not necessarily one billed segment. Long messages can use multiple segments, and character encoding can affect how many fit in a segment. Ask which incoming and outgoing texts count, rather than assuming every notification has the same charge.

ViFi's plan allowances and extra-usage rates apply to eligible segments under its [Messaging Policy](/legal/messaging-policy/). The [billing documentation](https://docs.vifi.us/billing/plans-and-pricing/) distinguishes messages on your ViFi number from personal sign-in codes and call-summary notifications.

If you do not plan to use eligible SMS, enter zero in the estimator. Do not convert a count of messages into segments without checking their actual length and encoding.

## Decide what should happen at the limit

A spending alert and a hard stop have different consequences. An alert tells your team to review usage. A stop-limit can prevent more chargeable calls from being handled.

ViFi's [usage and spending guardrails](https://docs.vifi.us/billing/usage-and-guardrails/) describe both. Choose an alert with time to respond, and agree on the caller experience before enabling a stop-limit. The lowest bill is not useful if the setup interrupts the calls you intended to cover.
