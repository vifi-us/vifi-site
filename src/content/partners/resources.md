## Short description (about 50 words)

ViFi is an AI phone assistant for small businesses. It answers every call,
day or night, has a natural conversation with the caller, and sends you a
summary, transcript, and recording the moment the call ends. You keep your
number, forward your calls, and you're live in minutes. Free 7-day trial, no
credit card.

## Long description (about 150 words)

Every missed call is a customer who dialed the next listing. ViFi makes sure
that stops happening. It is an AI phone assistant built for small businesses
that run on the phone: home services, practices, law firms, real estate, and
anyone who cannot always pick up.

When a call comes in, ViFi answers with your business name, listens, and
responds using what you told it about your business. It captures who called,
what they need, and how to reach them, answers common questions from your
knowledge base, and can take messages and send texts. The moment the call
ends you get a briefing: a short summary, the full transcript, and a
recording. It remembers repeat callers, so the second conversation picks up
where the first left off.

Setup is call forwarding from your existing number, so callers notice
nothing. Plans start at $49 a month with a 7-day free trial and no credit
card required.
