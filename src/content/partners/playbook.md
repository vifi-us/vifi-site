## The one-sentence pitch

**ViFi answers a small business's phone when nobody else can, has a real
conversation with the caller, and sends the owner a recap the moment the
call ends.** No phone tree, no hold music, no missed jobs.

The buyer already knows the problem. They miss calls while they are on a
roof, in an exam room, in court, or at dinner, and every missed call is a
customer who phoned the next listing. You are not selling AI. You are selling
the calls they are losing today.

## Who buys

The best fits are businesses that live on the phone and cannot always
answer it:

- **Home services:** HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, landscaping,
  cleaning, pest control. The owner is the technician.
- **Practices:** dental, medical, veterinary, chiropractic, physical therapy.
  Front desks get overwhelmed at peak times and the phone goes to voicemail
  after 5.
- **Professional services:** law firms, accountants, real-estate agents,
  insurance agencies. Every inbound call is a lead with a budget.
- **Anyone paying for an answering service** and unhappy with the cost or
  the scripts.

Warning signs it is not a fit yet: a business with a full-time receptionist
who never misses a call, or one that needs to transfer calls into a complex
internal phone system on day one.

## What ViFi actually does

Keep it concrete. Say these, in this order:

1. Callers reach a natural voice that answers with the business name,
   listens, and responds to questions using what the owner told it about the
   business.
2. It captures what the caller needs: who they are, what they want, when,
   and how to reach them. It can answer questions from the business's
   knowledge base, take messages, and send texts.
3. The owner gets a recap after every call: a short summary, the transcript,
   and a recording, by email and text.
4. It remembers repeat callers, so the second call picks up where the first
   left off.
5. Setup is call forwarding from the existing number. The business keeps its
   number and callers notice nothing. Most owners are live in minutes.

Everything above is on [vifi.us/features](/features) and
[how it works](/how-it-works). Do not promise anything that is not there.

## The demo that closes

Owners believe their ears, not a slide deck.

1. Open [vifi.us](/) with them and use the voice widget in the corner to talk
   to the ViFi agent live. Ask it something a real caller would ask.
2. Point out the recap: summary, transcript, recording. That is the part they
   did not know they wanted.
3. Ask: "What happened to the last call you missed?" Let them answer. Then
   show the [pricing page](/pricing): the Starter plan costs less than one
   missed job.
4. Send them your link, or give them your partner code. The trial is 7 days,
   free, no card, and your code takes 50% off their first month when they
   subscribe. They can hear their own agent take a test call before anything
   is billed.

## Objections you will hear

**"Callers will hate talking to a robot."** Let them hear it. Then explain
that the alternative today is voicemail, and most callers hang up on
voicemail. A natural voice that actually helps beats silence.

**"I already have someone who answers."** ViFi covers the calls that person
cannot: after hours, weekends, lunch, sick days, and the second line when
the first is busy. It is overflow and after-hours coverage, not a
replacement.

**"It's too expensive."** Compare it to one missed customer, not to zero.
Answering services with humans typically cost several times more per month
and read from scripts. The Starter plan is $49, and with your code the first
month is half that.

**"I don't have time to set this up."** Forwarding takes two minutes. The
setup wizard asks about the business in plain language. They can start with
just the greeting and the knowledge base and add more later.

**"What if it says something wrong?"** The agent only knows what the owner
tells it, and every call comes with a transcript, so the owner sees exactly
what was said and can fix the instructions in a minute. Recordings can be
turned off.

**"Is my data safe?"** Point them at the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy)
and the [Help Center](https://docs.vifi.us). Do not improvise security or
compliance claims; if they need specifics, ask them to email
<!--email_off-->support@vifi.us<!--/email_off-->.

## Where partners find customers

- Businesses you already advise: agencies, IT consultants, bookkeepers, and
  web designers can add "phone coverage" to every onboarding checklist.
- Local business groups, trade associations, chambers, and franchise
  networks in the verticals above.
- Content: "how to stop missing calls" guides, comparisons of answering
  services, and walkthrough videos. Put your link and your partner code in
  the description.
- Newsletters and communities for small-business owners and the trades.
