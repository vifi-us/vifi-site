## The Program

The Program lets you earn a commission when a business you refer becomes a
paying ViFi customer. Referrals are tracked, and commissions are calculated
and paid, through our affiliate platform provider, Tolt. You manage your
links, codes, statistics, payout details, and tax forms in the partner portal
at [partners.vifi.us](https://partners.vifi.us).

## Eligibility and enrollment

You must be at least 18 years old and able to enter into a binding
agreement. Partner accounts are currently accepted from the United States,
the United Kingdom, and Canada. We review every application and may approve
or decline it at our discretion, and we may ask for your website, audience,
or intended promotion methods before approving. One partner account per
person or business.

## Commissions

- **Rate.** You earn 20% of the net subscription and usage payments ViFi
  actually receives from each referred customer.
- **Duration.** Commissions continue for as long as the referred customer
  remains a paying ViFi customer.
- **Net payments.** Commissions are calculated on amounts received after
  refunds, chargebacks, credits, discounts, and taxes. A refund, chargeback,
  or failed payment reverses the related commission; if it has already been
  paid, we may deduct it from a future payout.
- **What does not earn a commission.** Free trials, unpaid invoices, your
  own purchases, purchases by your business or household, and customers who
  were already ViFi customers or in an active sales conversation with ViFi
  before your referral.

## Attribution

- A referral is credited when a visitor arrives through your referral link
  (or uses your partner code at checkout) and later creates a workspace that
  becomes a paying customer.
- Referral links are tracked for 90 days from the visit. If more than one
  partner refers the same customer, the first partner whose link the customer
  used receives the credit.
- Attribution disputes must be raised within 60 days of the customer's first
  payment. Our records, and those of our affiliate platform, are final.

## Payouts

- Commissions are paid monthly on net-30 terms: a commission earned in one
  month becomes payable after a 30-day hold and is paid with the next
  monthly payout run.
- Payouts are made through the partner portal by PayPal, Wise, or bank wire.
  Provider fees for your chosen method are deducted from the payout.
- There is currently no minimum payout amount.
- Before your first payout you must complete the tax form requested in the
  portal (IRS Form W-9 for U.S. partners; Form W-8BEN or W-8BEN-E otherwise)
  and provide valid payout details. You are responsible for all taxes on
  commissions you receive.

## How you may promote ViFi

You may share your referral link and partner code on your website, in your
newsletter, in content you create, in conversations with businesses you
advise, and on social media. You may use the logos, banners, and copy in the
[partner marketing kit](/partners/resources) in the form we provide them,
with the changes we permit there. When you do, you must:

- describe ViFi accurately and only make claims that appear on vifi.us or in
  the marketing kit;
- clearly disclose that you may earn a commission wherever a disclosure is
  required, including under the FTC Endorsement Guides in the United States
  and equivalent rules where you operate;
- comply with all laws that apply to your promotion, including anti-spam,
  consumer-protection, and privacy laws.

## What is not allowed

The following end your participation and forfeit unpaid commissions:

- referring yourself, your own business, your household, or accounts you
  control, or creating accounts on behalf of customers;
- bidding on "ViFi", "vifi.us", or misspellings of them in paid search or
  paid social, or using them in ad display URLs;
- registering domains, social handles, or app-store listings that contain the
  ViFi name, or presenting yourself as ViFi;
- unsolicited email or text messages, comment spam, bulk or automated
  posting, or any promotion your audience did not opt in to;
- coupon, deal, cashback, or loyalty sites and browser extensions, and
  offering cash or other incentives for signing up, unless we approve it in
  writing;
- placing referral cookies without a genuine click ("cookie stuffing"),
  framing vifi.us, or using pop-ups, redirects, or hidden links;
- misleading, defamatory, obscene, or discriminatory content, or promotion
  alongside it;
- promising outcomes, features, prices, or terms that ViFi has not published.
